## 1. Introduction
### Problem ###
Student dropout affects both the reputation of the educational institution and the personal development and future opportunities of students. One significant risk factor for dropout is low academic performance, but there is no clear overview of which course grades are correlated with dropout. The lack of this knowledge limits the implementation of effective early interventions and support measures.

### Objective ###


To analyze students’ course-specific academic performance in order to identify whether passing specific courses or achieving certain grades is directly associated with the risk of dropout.
